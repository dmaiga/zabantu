This page explains which personal data the website zabantu.net collects, why, how long it is kept and how to exercise your rights. It is written in accordance with Malian legislation on the protection of personal data, under the supervision of the Personal Data Protection Authority (APDP).

1. Data controller

Zabantu research group, Faculty of Economics and Management (FSEG), Badalabougou University Campus, Bamako, Mali. E-mail: Info@zabantu.net · Phone: +223 77 94 14 70 / +223 76 63 45 15. See also the legal notice.

3. Legal basis

  • Your consent: newsletter subscription, photo and biography of the public profile.
  • Your request: answering a contact message, reviewing an application or a training enrolment, managing your account.
  • Our legitimate interest: website security and protection against automated submissions (bots).

4. Publicly visible information

If your application is accepted, your member profile (name, photo, biography, position) is displayed on the “Members” page. You can ask for it to be changed or removed at any time. Publications display the names of their authors.

5. Recipients

  • The moderation team (Zabantu group): these moderators manage the website (memberships, messages, publications, newsletter) and are the only people authorised to process your data on our behalf.
  • Our technical providers:
    • LWS (France): our host for the website and e-mail.
    • Our web developer: acts only on our instructions, for technical maintenance and for your requests for changes or deletion.
  • External display services: when pages load, third-party tools (Google Fonts, Cloudflare, jsDelivr, jQuery) automatically receive your technical data and IP address (to find out more, see our cookie policy).

Your data is not disclosed to any other third party, unless required by law.

6. Transfers outside Mali

The external content services mentioned above are operated from abroad. The data from your forms and your account is stored in France, on the servers of our host LWS.

7. Security

Connections to the website are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored in encrypted form, access to the dashboard is restricted to authorised people and forms are protected against automated submissions. No system is infallible: in the event of a data breach affecting you, we would inform you and the APDP, as required by law.

8. Your rights

You have the following rights over your data:

  • access: find out which data we hold about you and obtain a copy;
  • rectification: have inaccurate data corrected;
  • erasure: ask for your data to be deleted;
  • objection: object, on legitimate grounds, to processing;
  • withdrawal of consent at any time.

How to exercise them: write to Info@zabantu.net, stating the purpose of your request and the e-mail address concerned. The moderators handle your request and, if a technical operation is needed, have it carried out by the website developer. We may ask you for proof of identity if in doubt. We reply within one month.

To unsubscribe from the newsletter, use the link at the bottom of each message: unsubscription is immediate.

If you believe that your rights are not being respected, you can refer the matter to Mali's Personal Data Protection Authority (APDP).

9. Updates

This policy may change. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page.
