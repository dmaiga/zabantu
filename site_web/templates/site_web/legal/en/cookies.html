A cookie is a small file stored on your device when you visit a website. The website zabantu.net uses no advertising or social network cookies and no audience measurement cookies. It only uses technical cookies that are essential for it to work: they do not require your consent. A banner informs you of this on your first visit; the “Manage my cookies” link at the bottom of each page reopens it at any time.

1. Cookies set by the website

CookiePurposeDuration
csrftokenProtects forms against fraudulent submissions.1 year at most
zabantu_consentRemembers that you have read the information banner, so that it is not shown again.6 months
sessionidKeeps you logged in (team management area or the “My space” training area) and remembers the step reached in some forms.Length of the session (2 weeks at most)

Visitors who do not log in only receive the form protection cookie, when they open a page that contains a form.

2. External services loaded by the pages

To be displayed, the pages load fonts, icons and libraries from external services: jsDelivr, Cloudflare (cdnjs), Google Fonts and the jQuery CDN. Your browser sends them your IP address and technical information in order to receive these files. We do not share any other data with them and do not derive any statistics from them.

The links to LinkedIn, Facebook and the WhatsApp channel are plain links: these services receive no information unless you click on them. Once on their website, their own policy applies.

3. Managing or refusing cookies

You can delete or block cookies at any time in your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge: “Privacy” section). Blocking technical cookies may prevent you from logging in and from sending forms.

4. Find out more

See the privacy policy for your rights and for how to contact us: Info@zabantu.net.
